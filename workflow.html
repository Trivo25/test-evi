Instructions

Compare the obligations in the agreement with its addenda and supporting schedules. Keep exact deadlines and triggering events. Quote each obligation from a specific source version. Flag missing information as an open question. Do not decide which document prevails.

Output format

  • Requirements comparison
    One row per obligation. One column per source. Every cell cites an evidence identifier or states “Not addressed”.
  • Review brief
    Numbered sections, one per obligation. Each statement carries an inline citation. Open questions are listed, not resolved.

Review criteria

  • Deadline support. Every deadline in an output is stated in its cited passage. A deadline without a passage is flagged.
  • Distinct triggering events. Events such as discovery and confirmation are quoted exactly and never merged.
  • Missing or conflicting information. Gaps and conflicts between sources are listed as open questions. The output does not choose which source prevails.
  • Human review. Every output stays “Human review pending” until a person approves it. Automated checks never grant approval.
