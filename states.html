Reference

Assessment statuses

Every status pairs an icon with a label. None shows a confidence percentage or a document score. Three questions stay separate: does the citation open the correct passage, does the passage support the statement, has a human approved the draft.

  • Passage supports statement
  • Partially supported
  • Needs manual verificationUsed when the passage is a table, a scanned page, or a cross-reference the check cannot read.
  • Draft changed · Needs recheck
  • Source replaced · Needs recheck
  • Source removed
  • Human review pendingShown on the draft. No automated result changes it.
